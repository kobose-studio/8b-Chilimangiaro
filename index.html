MANIFESTO

8B & I CHILIMANGIARO
Magma Roots Ensemble.

We don’t play music; we trigger eruptions.

Chilimangiaro is a collective born to deconstruct the boundaries of sound. We take the solid foundations of Reggae Roots and Roots Lovers and melt them into a flow of World, Soul, Funk and Jazz.

Guided by the creative vision of 8B, our Dub excursions are not just a style, but a physical space: the Cratere Sonoro (Sonic Crater). It’s a place where low frequencies dictate the law and the "Levare" rhythm is the heartbeat of a collective ritual.

No borders. No clichés. Just Magma.
