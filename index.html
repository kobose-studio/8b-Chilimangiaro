8B & I CHILIMANGIARO

/¯\ Magma Roots Ensemble /¯\

I Chilimangiaro is a collective born to deconstruct the boundaries of sound. With Roots Reggae as the absolute protagonist, we take the solid foundations of Roots Lovers, transforming them into an organic flow of Tropical Magma, Dub & Jazzy-Funky Ska Soul.

We call this vital energy eRoots (Earth Roots): a deep connection to the planet and to human brotherhood, translated into heavy grooves and conscious lightness. It’s our way of honoring the earth, celebrating it with playfulness and a vibrant joy of Being.

Inside the Sonic Crater (Cratere Sonoro)—a physical and mental space shaped by 8B’s sonic coordinates and brought to life by the band's interplay—low frequencies dictate the law. Here, the "Levare" rhythm becomes the heartbeat of a collective ritual.

No borders. No gurus. No clichés. Just Magma.
